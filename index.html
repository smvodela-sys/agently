Agently combines the power of large language models with practical automation tools — all running privately on your device.

🤖

On-Device Gemma AI

Powered by Google's Gemma 4 language model running entirely on your iPhone or Android. No internet required for inference — your queries never leave your device.

⏰

Scheduled Agents

Set agents to run automatically — daily morning briefings, price alerts, habit reminders. Your AI works in the background and notifies you when it's done.

🔍

Real-Time Web Search

Agents can search the web for live information — trending topics, news, local businesses, stock prices, and crypto rates — then synthesise it into a clear answer.

📅

Calendar & Reminders

How It Works

Up and running in minutes

No configuration files. No developer setup. Just download, describe what you want, and let your agent get to work.

1

Download & Install

Get Agently from the App Store or Google Play. The on-device Gemma model installs automatically in the background.

2

Create an Agent

Tap Create, describe your goal in plain English — "give me the top 5 trending news stories every morning" — and choose a schedule.

3

Agent Gets to Work

At the scheduled time your agent wakes up, searches the web, analyses the results, and writes you a clear, concise summary.

4

Tap the Notification

Receive a push notification when your agent finishes. Tap it to read the result, share it, or trigger a follow-up run.
